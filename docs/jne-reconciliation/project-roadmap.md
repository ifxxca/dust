## Activation and delivery

Business unknowns are configuration/UAT gates, not incomplete generic services. B1–B4/B6–B8 must be recorded with real evidence, verification actor and scope before production activation. M4 confirmed authority/closure rules remain enforced independently.

All new flags default false. This continuation does not migrate the operational database, change live ownership/POP, enable schedules or contact live JNE destructively. Use an isolated UAT database/report disk and disabled schedules first. See uat-plan.md and uat-readiness.md for one consolidated acceptance and rollout package.

Each major increment has its own commit. No new infrastructure or dependencies were added. Reversible technical work is complete; operational certification remains explicitly pending.
